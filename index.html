Privacy Policy for Strong Vault

Last updated: August 2026

Strong Vault ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection and Storage

Strong Vault stores all your data — including passwords, notes, documents, and any other content you save — locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your vault data on external servers.

No Data Sharing

Since your data never leaves your device, we do not share, sell, or transmit your personal information to any third parties.

Biometric and Authentication Data

If you use biometric authentication (fingerprint, face recognition) or a PIN to secure the app, this authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system. Strong Vault does not have access to, collect, or store your biometric data.

Permissions

The App may request certain device permissions (such as storage access) solely to enable core functionality like saving and retrieving your vault contents. These permissions are used only on-device and not for data collection.

Children's Privacy

Strong Vault does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
